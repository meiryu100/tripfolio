**India is the most populous country in the world, followed by China, the United States, Indonesia and Pakistan.** In the UN's 2024 revision of its World Population Prospects, India had about 1.44 billion people on 1 July 2023, just ahead of China at about 1.42 billion. Together those two hold roughly 35% of everyone alive. This guide gives the full top 12 from one consistent source, explains why other lists disagree, and shows how the biggest countries look on a travel map.

## What are the most populous countries in the world?

Here is the top 12, using the UN's mid-year estimates for 1 July 2023 as shown in [Wikipedia's list of countries by population (United Nations)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_countries_by_population_(United_Nations)). Figures are rounded to the nearest million.

1. **India**: about 1,438 million
2. **China**: about 1,423 million
3. **United States**: about 343 million
4. **Indonesia**: about 281 million
5. **Pakistan**: about 248 million
6. **Nigeria**: about 228 million
7. **Brazil**: about 211 million
8. **Bangladesh**: about 171 million
9. **Russia**: about 145 million
10. **Mexico**: about 130 million
11. **Ethiopia**: about 129 million
12. **Japan**: about 124 million

The same source puts the world total at about 8.09 billion for mid-2023. By my own arithmetic from those figures, the twelve countries above hold about 60% of the world's people. The other 183 countries on Tripfolio's 195-country list share the remaining 40%.

## Which country has the largest population?

India. The UN figures show India already slightly ahead of China in 2022, by about 244,000 people, and the gap grew to roughly 15 million by 2023 because China's population fell while India's kept growing.
Two details are worth knowing:

- **"China" in these tables is mainland China.** The UN list shows Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan as separate rows. Hong Kong (about 7.4 million) and Macao (about 0.7 million) are labeled "(China)", and Taiwan is listed on its own with a footnote.
- **The gap is small compared with the uncertainty.** Population figures are estimates built from censuses and models, so the exact moment one country overtook the other depends on whose numbers you trust.

## Why do population rankings differ between sources?

Search for this topic and you will see slightly different top-12 lists. There are three reasons.

### Different dates

A population is a moving number. [Wikipedia's list of countries and dependencies by population](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_countries_and_dependencies_by_population) mixes figures from different dates, from a 2022 census result for Bangladesh to a 2026 national projection for Indonesia. A list that mixes dates will never match one that fixes a single day.

### Different methods

Some lists use each government's own estimate, others use the UN's model. Pakistan and Bangladesh are shown there by census results, while others rely on projections. The UN model smooths everything onto one date so countries can be compared fairly, which is why this guide uses it.

### Different definitions of the country

Whether Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan are part of "China" changes the total, and so does whether a territory counts as a country. Tripfolio keeps a clean rule for this: only the 195 sovereign countries count toward your total, and territories are colored on the map but not counted.

The top 5 are stable across every source. Past about eighth place, the order can shift by one or two places depending on the list, so treat positions 9 to 12 as "roughly in this range" rather than fixed.

## Population is not the same as visitors

Population says how many people live in a country, not how many travelers visit it. If you are counting countries for fun, it gives you a hint about scale, nothing more. Lists of tiny places are a better challenge for a completionist: see [the smallest countries in the world](/blog/smallest-countries-in-the-world), or compare with [the largest countries by area](/blog/largest-countries-in-the-world). The biggest by people and the biggest by land overlap only partly. Russia, China, the United States and Brazil appear in both top lists, but Canada, Australia and Kazakhstan are enormous in area and not close to the population top 12.

## FAQ

### Which is the most populous country in the world?

India, with about 1.44 billion people in the UN's 1 July 2023 estimate, slightly ahead of China at about 1.42 billion.

### What are the top 5 most populous countries?

India, China, the United States, Indonesia and Pakistan, in that order, in the UN's World Population Prospects 2024 figures for 2023.

### Did India really overtake China?

In the UN's figures, India was already marginally ahead in 2022 and clearly ahead in 2023, because China's population was shrinking while India's was still growing.

### Do population lists include Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan in China?

The UN list shows Hong Kong and Macao as separate rows labeled "(China)", and Taiwan as its own row with a footnote, so the China figure refers to the mainland.

### Why do different websites show different rankings?

They use different dates, different data sources (census, national estimate or UN model) and different rules about which territories count, so the order can shift below the top five.
