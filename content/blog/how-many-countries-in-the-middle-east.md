There are **about 17 countries in the Middle East**, but the number depends on who is counting. There is no official United Nations definition of the region, and the common lists run from 16 to 18. Tripfolio's map counts **16**, because it files Cyprus under Europe. This guide gives you the full list, shows exactly which places make the number move, and explains how to count your own Middle East trips.

Unlike Europe or Africa, the Middle East is not a continent. It is a political and cultural region whose borders were drawn by history, not by geography. That is why a simple question has a few honest answers.

## How many countries are in the Middle East?

The most common answer comes from Wikipedia's [Middle East article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Middle_East), which describes the region as covering all of Egypt and all of West Asia except the Caucasus. Its list has **17 UN-recognized countries**: 16 UN member states plus the State of Palestine, which is a UN observer. It also lists one British Overseas Territory, Akrotiri and Dhekelia, on Cyprus, which is not a country.

Those 17 are:

- Bahrain
- Cyprus
- Egypt
- Iran
- Iraq
- Israel
- Jordan
- Kuwait
- Lebanon
- Oman
- Palestine
- Qatar
- Saudi Arabia
- Syria
- Türkiye
- the United Arab Emirates
- Yemen

Add the Caucasus countries, Armenia, Azerbaijan and Georgia, and many lists reach 20. Take Cyprus out and you have 16. All three answers are in use.

## Is there an official list of Middle Eastern countries?

No. The term "Middle East" is a Western invention. Wikipedia traces it to the British India Office in the 1850s, and it spread after the American naval strategist Alfred Thayer Mahan used it in a 1902 article. Governments and organizations have used very different versions since.

Two examples show how loose it is:

- A 1958 US State Department definition listed just ten countries: Egypt, Syria, Israel, Lebanon, Jordan, Iraq, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Bahrain and Qatar.
- The 1957 Eisenhower Doctrine used a far wider area, from Libya in the west to Pakistan in the east, plus Sudan and Ethiopia.

The United Nations avoids the term for its statistics. Its [standard country and area codes](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/methodology/m49/) have no "Middle East" group. They use **Western Asia** instead, which is a geographic block and not a political idea.

## What does the UN list as Western Asia?

The UN's Western Asia group has **18 entries**: Armenia, Azerbaijan, Bahrain, Cyprus, Georgia, Iraq, Israel, Jordan, Kuwait, Lebanon, Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, the State of Palestine, the Syrian Arab Republic, Türkiye, the United Arab Emirates and Yemen.

Compare that with the Middle East list above and you can see the two differences:

1. **Egypt and Iran are not in Western Asia.** The UN puts Egypt in Northern Africa and Iran in Southern Asia.
2. **Armenia, Azerbaijan and Georgia are in Western Asia**, but most Middle East lists leave out the Caucasus.

The UN also notes that its groupings are made for statistical convenience and do not imply any opinion about the legal status of a country or territory. We follow the same spirit on the map.

## Why does Tripfolio count 16?

Tripfolio works from a fixed list of **195 sovereign countries**, the same list its [guide to how many countries there are in the world](/blog/how-many-countries-are-there-in-the-world) explains, and each country sits on exactly one continent. That means a "Middle East" label cannot be a separate group, because Egypt would have to be in Africa and in the Middle East at once.

So for this article we take the commonly used Middle East list, and check each country against the map:

- **Egypt** is filed under Africa (Northern Africa). It is mostly in Africa, with the Sinai Peninsula in Asia.
- **Iran, Iraq, Israel, Jordan, Kuwait, Lebanon, Oman, Palestine, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, Syria, Türkiye, the United Arab Emirates, Bahrain and Yemen** are filed under Asia (Western Asia, apart from Iran in Southern Asia).
- **Cyprus** is filed under Europe, as the island is a European Union member.

Remove Cyprus from the 17 and you get the 16 on our map. All 16 count toward your total out of 195.

## Which countries are in the Middle East, by sub-region?

It helps to split the region into groups that travelers already use.

### The Arabian Peninsula

Saudi Arabia, Yemen, Oman, the United Arab Emirates, Qatar, Bahrain and Kuwait. Saudi Arabia is the largest country in the region by area, according to Wikipedia. The [United Arab Emirates](/countries/united-arab-emirates) is the one most travelers pass through on a stopover.

### The Levant

Israel, Jordan, Lebanon, Syria and Palestine. [Israel](/countries/israel) and [Jordan](/countries/jordan) are often combined in a single trip.

### Mesopotamia and Iran

Iraq and Iran. Iran is one of the three most populous countries of the region, along with Egypt and Türkiye.

### Egypt and Türkiye

[Egypt](/countries/egypt) and [Türkiye](/countries/turkiye) are the two countries that connect the Middle East to other regions. Egypt spans two continents, and Türkiye has a small part, East Thrace, in Europe.

## What about Egypt, Türkiye and Cyprus?

These three account for most of the confusion.

- **Egypt.** Wikipedia says Egypt lies almost entirely in North Africa, which is why the UN does not call it Western Asia. It is still part of the Middle East in almost every definition, because of its history and its political ties. On Tripfolio's map it counts as an African country, which also means it counts once in [how many countries are in Africa](/blog/how-many-countries-in-africa).
- **Türkiye.** The UN lists it in Western Asia, and so does Tripfolio. Most of its land is in Asia.
- **Cyprus.** The UN puts it in Western Asia, and Wikipedia lists it as part of the Middle East. Tripfolio puts it in Europe, as explained in [how many countries are in Europe](/blog/how-many-countries-in-europe).

## Is Palestine counted as a country?

It depends on the list. The State of Palestine is a **UN observer**, not a full member, so it is not one of the 193 member states. Tripfolio includes it among its 195 sovereign countries, which means it is in the 16. If you only count UN members, the Middle East has 15 by our map and 16 with Cyprus.

For the general rule about what makes a place a country on the map, see [how many countries there are in the world](/blog/how-many-countries-are-there-in-the-world).

## Which territories show up on a Middle East map?

A few places in or near the region are not sovereign countries:

- **Akrotiri and Dhekelia**, two British military base areas on Cyprus, listed by Wikipedia as a British Overseas Territory.
- **Northern Cyprus**, which Wikipedia lists among non-UN states.
- **The Golan Heights**, which Wikipedia lists as occupied.

Tripfolio does not add these as separate countries. They are not part of the 195, so they do not change your total.

## How to count your own Middle East trips

Pick one rule and keep it. There are three sensible ones:

1. **16, the Tripfolio map.** Egypt counts for Africa and Cyprus for Europe, so every country is counted once.
2. **17, the Wikipedia list.** Use it if you want Cyprus and Egypt in the Middle East.
3. **18, the UN Western Asia list.** Use it if you want to include the Caucasus too, but note that it leaves out Egypt and Iran.

If you are not sure whether a short stop counts at all, read [what counts as visiting a country](/blog/what-counts-as-visiting-a-country). A layover in Dubai or Doha, with no passport stamp, is a classic gray area.

Before you plan a trip, check your government's current travel advice. Some countries in the region have conditions that change quickly, and entry rules can change from one year to the next.

## FAQ

### How many countries are in the Middle East?

Most lists give 17: 16 UN member states plus the State of Palestine. Counts run from 16 to 18 depending on whether Cyprus and the Caucasus are included. Tripfolio's map counts 16.

### Is Egypt in the Middle East or Africa?

Both. Egypt lies almost entirely in North Africa, but it is part of the Middle East in almost every definition. The UN lists it in Northern Africa, and so does Tripfolio.

### Is Türkiye in the Middle East?

Yes, in most definitions. The UN lists Türkiye in Western Asia, though a small part of the country, East Thrace, is in Europe.

### What is the difference between the Middle East and Western Asia?

Western Asia is a geographic grouping used by the UN, with 18 entries. The Middle East is a political term with no official definition, and it usually includes Egypt and Iran but not the Caucasus.

### Is Israel in the Middle East?

Yes. Israel is a UN member and is part of the Middle East in every common definition. The UN lists it in Western Asia, and so does Tripfolio.
