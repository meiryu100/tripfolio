There are **48 countries in Asia** by the most common count, which follows the United Nations geographic regions. That number includes the State of Palestine and Cyprus. Count only UN member states and you get **47**, because Palestine is not a UN member. Tripfolio's map also shows **47**, for a slightly different reason: it files Cyprus under Europe and keeps Palestine in Asia.

Asia is the biggest continent and the one where the answer depends most on where you draw the border. This guide gives you the full list by region, explains every place that makes the number wobble, and shows how to count your own Asian countries without second-guessing.

## How many countries are in Asia by the UN count?

The United Nations Statistics Division publishes a [standard list of country and area codes](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/methodology/m49/) grouped into regions. Its Asia section has **50 entries**. Two of them, Hong Kong and Macao, are special administrative regions of China, not countries. That leaves 48 countries, which is also the figure [Worldometers](https://www.worldometers.info/geography/how-many-countries-in-asia/) gives for Asia, citing the UN.

Those 48 are spread over five sub-regions: 5 in Central Asia, 7 in Eastern Asia (5 countries plus the 2 regions above), 11 in South-eastern Asia, 9 in Southern Asia and 18 in Western Asia.

## Why does Tripfolio count 47?

Two small choices explain the difference between 48 and 47.

- **Cyprus.** The UN puts Cyprus in Western Asia, because the island lies close to Türkiye, Syria and Lebanon. But Cyprus is a member of the European Union, and most travelers think of it as Europe. Tripfolio files it under Europe, which is why it appears in our [guide to how many countries are in Europe](/blog/how-many-countries-in-europe).
- **Palestine.** Tripfolio counts the State of Palestine as one of its 195 sovereign countries, so it stays in Asia.

Take Cyprus out of the UN's 48 and you land on 47. Either way, nobody is double counted: each country sits on exactly one continent on the map.

## What is the full list of Asian countries?

Here are the 47 countries, grouped the way Tripfolio's map groups them.

### Central Asia (5)

Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan, Turkmenistan and Uzbekistan.

### Eastern Asia (5)

China, Japan, Mongolia, North Korea and South Korea.

### South-eastern Asia (11)

Brunei, Cambodia, Indonesia, Laos, Malaysia, Myanmar, the Philippines, Singapore, Thailand, Timor-Leste and Vietnam.

### Southern Asia (9)

Afghanistan, Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, Iran, Maldives, Nepal, Pakistan and Sri Lanka.

### Western Asia (17)

Armenia, Azerbaijan, Bahrain, Georgia, Iraq, Israel, Jordan, Kuwait, Lebanon, Oman, Palestine, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, Syria, Türkiye, the United Arab Emirates and Yemen.

Add Cyprus to the Western Asia group if you follow the UN regions, and you have the 48.

## Which Asian places are territories, not countries?

Three places in Eastern Asia show up on Tripfolio's map as territories. You can color them in, but they do not count toward your total out of 195:

- **Hong Kong**, a special administrative region of China.
- **Macau**, also a special administrative region of China. The UN spells it Macao.
- **Taiwan**, which the UN does not list as a separate country. Its status is a political question, and we simply follow the UN list so that every traveler is counted the same way.

Marking Taiwan on your map is still a perfectly good way to remember the trip. It just sits in the "places" column, not the "countries" column. For the general rule behind this, see [how many countries there are in the world](/blog/how-many-countries-are-there-in-the-world).

## Is Russia in Asia or Europe?

Russia stretches across both. Most of its land is in Asia, but most of its people live in the European part, and the UN files it under Eastern Europe. Tripfolio does the same, so Russia counts as a European country on your map and not in the 47.

If you have crossed the Urals on the Trans-Siberian railway, you can still say you have been to Asia. You just will not get a second country out of it.

## Are Türkiye, Georgia, Armenia and Azerbaijan in Asia?

Yes, on the UN list all four are in Western Asia. This is the corner of the map where Europe and Asia blur:

- [Türkiye](/countries/turkiye) has a famous city, Istanbul, split by the Bosphorus. Most of the country is in Asia.
- [Georgia](/countries/georgia), Armenia and Azerbaijan sit in the Caucasus and are usually counted in Asia by the UN, though all three have strong ties to Europe.

Kazakhstan is a similar case. A small part of it lies west of the Ural River, but the UN lists it in Central Asia, and so do we.

## Which number should I use for my own count?

Pick one rule and stay with it:

1. **47, the Tripfolio map.** Easy to match to a world total of 195, and Cyprus is counted once, under Europe.
2. **48, the UN regions.** Use it if you want Cyprus to count as an Asian country.
3. **47 or 48, plus bonus places.** Keep Hong Kong, Macau and Taiwan on the side as places you have been, without adding them to the country count.

How you count matters less than being consistent. If you are unsure whether a short stop counts at all, read [what counts as visiting a country](/blog/what-counts-as-visiting-a-country).

## Where to start if you have been to few Asian countries

Asia is where many travelers have the most room to grow their map, and it is full of easy first trips. A few countries with their own pages on Tripfolio:

- [Japan](/countries/japan), for cities, trains and food, with an excellent transport system

- [India](/countries/india), the biggest country in Southern Asia, with enough to fill several trips

Check your government's current travel advice before you book. Entry rules and conditions change from country to country and from year to year.

## FAQ

### How many countries are in Asia?

There are 48 countries in Asia on the UN list, counting Cyprus and the State of Palestine. Only UN member states give you 47, which is also the number on Tripfolio's map.

### Is Russia in Asia?

Russia spans both continents, but the UN lists it in Eastern Europe, and so does Tripfolio. It is not part of the 47 Asian countries.

### Is Cyprus in Asia or Europe?

Geographically it lies in Asia, and the UN lists it in Western Asia. It is a European Union member, so many lists count it as Europe. Tripfolio files it under Europe.

### Is Taiwan a country?

The UN does not list Taiwan as a separate country, so Tripfolio shows it as a territory. You can still mark it as visited, but it does not count toward your total out of 195.

### Do Hong Kong and Macau count as countries?

No. Both are special administrative regions of China. You can mark them on the map, but they do not count toward your country total.
