Here is the alphabetical list of countries: **195 countries**, from Afghanistan to Zimbabwe, grouped by first letter below. That is the 193 member states of the United Nations plus the 2 UN observer states, the Holy See (Vatican City) and Palestine. **S has the most countries (26)**, M and B come next, and no country starts with W or X.

One warning before the list: the order depends on the name you use. Is it Ivory Coast or Côte d'Ivoire? Eswatini or Swaziland? Each choice moves a country to a different letter. The list uses the everyday English names Tripfolio uses, and the sections after it explain where other lists differ.

## How many countries are there in the world?

[Wikipedia's list of sovereign states](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_sovereign_states) shows 193 UN member states and two UN General Assembly non-member observer states. [Its page on UN member states](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Member_states_of_the_United_Nations) gives the same split and names the two observers: the Holy See and Palestine.

Tripfolio counts those same 195 as "countries", so the list below has 195 entries. Territories such as Greenland, Puerto Rico or Hong Kong, and states with limited recognition, are not in it. ## Which letters have the most and fewest countries?

Counted from the list above:

- **S: 26.** It leads by a wide margin, and three of its names start with "Saint".
- **M: 18**, then **B: 17** and **C: 15**.
- **N and G: 11 each**, the same as A and T.
- **O, Q and Y: one country each** (Oman, Qatar, Yemen).
- **W and X: none.** Nothing starts with W under any common English name, and none starts with X.

The totals add up: 11 + 17 + 15 + 5 + 8 + 3 + 11 + 3 + 9 + 3 + 5 + 9 + 18 + 11 + 1 + 10 + 1 + 4 + 26 + 11 + 7 + 4 + 1 + 2 = 195.

## Why different lists put the same country under different letters

There is no single official English alphabetical order, so the same country can move. These are the usual reasons.

### Countries that changed their name

- **Eswatini** was Swaziland until 19 April 2018, according to Wikipedia's page on UN member states. Old lists put it under S, new ones under E.
- **Czechia** is the short name that replaced "Czech Republic" in 2016 (the same page gives 17 May 2016). Both start with C, so the letter does not move, but the place within C does.
- **Türkiye** and **Turkey** both start with T, so the letter does not change. Our [Türkiye page](/countries/turkiye) uses the new spelling.

### Short names and long names

- **Ivory Coast or Côte d'Ivoire.** Wikipedia's page on UN member states lists it as Côte d'Ivoire and notes it was referred to as Ivory Coast until 31 December 1985. Under Ivory Coast it is an I; under Côte d'Ivoire it is a C. See our [Ivory Coast page](/countries/ivory-coast) for how we name it.
- **Cape Verde or Cabo Verde.** Cabo Verde asked in 2013 that its name not be translated. Both start with C.
- **Congo.** There are two: the Republic of the Congo, and the Democratic Republic of the Congo (called Zaire from 1971 to 1997). Short names ("Congo" and "DR Congo") can put one under C and the other under D, while the long names sit under R and D. We use "Republic of the Congo" (R) and "DR Congo" (D).
- **North Korea and South Korea** are listed under N and S here. Formal lists such as "Korea, Democratic People's Republic of" put them under K.

So on a different list the count for C, D, I and R could change by one or two, and the total stays 195.

### The word "the"

Names like "the Bahamas" and "the Gambia" are filed under B and G, not T. Most alphabetical lists ignore a leading "the", and so does this one.

## FAQ

### How many countries are in the alphabetical list of countries?

There are 195: the 193 UN member states plus the two UN observer states, the Holy See (Vatican City) and Palestine. Territories and states with limited recognition are not in this count.

### Which letter has the most countries?

S has the most, with 26 countries in the list above. M comes second with 18, and B third with 17.

### Which letters have no countries?

No country starts with W or X. O, Q and Y have only one each: Oman, Qatar and Yemen.

### Why does my list of countries have a different order?

The order depends on which names are used. A list that says Côte d'Ivoire instead of Ivory Coast, or Swaziland instead of Eswatini, files the same country under a different letter.

### What is the first and last country alphabetically?

Afghanistan comes first and Zimbabwe last in this list.
