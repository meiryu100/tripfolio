There are **12 countries in South America**: Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Chile, Colombia, Ecuador, Guyana, Paraguay, Peru, Suriname, Uruguay and Venezuela. Every one of them is a sovereign state. The continent also has a few territories that belong to other countries, such as French Guiana and the Falkland Islands, and those are not counted as countries.

This guide gives you the full list with capitals, explains the territories and the edge cases, and shows how to keep count of the South American countries you have visited.

## How many countries are in South America?

Twelve. That is the figure you will find in atlases, in the [list of sovereign states and dependent territories in South America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_sovereign_states_and_dependent_territories_in_South_America), and on Tripfolio's map. Together they cover about 17.8 million square kilometers and more than 430 million people, according to [Wikipedia's South America article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_America) (population figure from 2021).

The United Nations' [standard country and area codes](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/methodology/m49/) list 16 entries in its South America group. Twelve are the countries above. The other four are territories: French Guiana, the Falkland Islands, South Georgia and the South Sandwich Islands, and Bouvet Island. That gap, 12 versus 16, is where most of the confusion comes from.

## What is the full list of South American countries?

Here are the 12, with their capitals.

- **Argentina**: Buenos Aires
- **Bolivia**: Sucre is the constitutional capital, and La Paz is the seat of government
- **Brazil**: Brasília
- **Chile**: Santiago
- **Colombia**: Bogotá
- **Ecuador**: Quito
- **Guyana**: Georgetown
- **Paraguay**: Asunción
- **Peru**: Lima
- **Suriname**: Paramaribo
- **Uruguay**: Montevideo
- **Venezuela**: Caracas

Area and population figures for each country are in the Wikipedia list linked above (population figures there are from 2021).

## Which South American places are territories, not countries?

Three dependencies are normally listed as part of South America, and the UN adds a fourth:

- **French Guiana** is an overseas region and department of France, on the northeast coast. It is part of the European Union as an outermost region.
- **The Falkland Islands** are a British Overseas Territory in the South Atlantic.
- **South Georgia and the South Sandwich Islands** are also a British Overseas Territory. The UN places them in South America, though they are sometimes grouped with Antarctica.
- **Bouvet Island** is an uninhabited island in the far South Atlantic. The UN lists it under South America as well.

You can color French Guiana and the Falkland Islands on Tripfolio's map, but they do not count toward your total of 195 sovereign countries. Our map files South Georgia and Bouvet Island under Antarctica, not South America. The rule behind this is the same everywhere: a sovereign country counts, a dependency does not. We explain the global total in [how many countries there are in the world](/blog/how-many-countries-are-there-in-the-world).

## Why do some people say South America has 13 countries?

Usually because French Guiana gets counted as a country. It looks like one on a map, with its own capital, Cayenne, and its own coast. But it is a full part of France, so it has no independent government and no seat at the UN.

A second source of the "13" or "14" is the Falkland Islands, which Argentina also claims under the name Islas Malvinas. The UN list carries the name "Falkland Islands (Malvinas)" for exactly this reason. If you are counting a trip there, treat it as a bonus place, not a country.

## Is Panama in South America?

No. Panama is in Central America, which is part of North America, so it does not count toward the 12. Colombia is the southern neighbor across the Darién region. If you are counting continents, our map keeps the two sides separate.

## How big are the countries?

Very different. Brazil is the giant: at about 8.5 million square kilometers it covers close to half the continent, and it is home to almost half of South Americans. Suriname and Guyana are the opposite, with fewer than a million people each. Uruguay and Suriname are among the smallest by area, at roughly 176,000 and 164,000 square kilometers.

Two of the twelve, Bolivia and Paraguay, are landlocked. Every other country touches the sea.

## Which languages are spoken?

Spanish is the main language in most countries, and Portuguese in Brazil. But the continent is more varied than that:

- **Suriname** speaks Dutch.
- **Guyana** speaks English.
- **French Guiana** speaks French, as part of France.
- **Quechua, Aymara and Guaraní** are spoken by many people in Peru, Bolivia and Paraguay, alongside Spanish.

That mix is part of why a trip across the continent feels like crossing several worlds, even when the border is a short bus ride.

## Does South America count as one continent or two?

In most English-speaking countries, the Americas are taught as two continents, North America and South America, and that is how the UN groups them in its regional tables. Some countries teach a single "America" continent. Tripfolio uses seven continents, so South America is one of the seven, and the progress counter on your profile shows how many of the seven you have reached.

## How do I count my South American countries?

Keep it simple:

1. **Count the 12 sovereign countries you have actually visited.** Setting foot in the country counts. If you are unsure about a quick stop, read [what counts as visiting a country](/blog/what-counts-as-visiting-a-country).
2. **Keep territories on the side.** A day in the Falkland Islands or French Guiana is worth remembering, but it adds to your places, not your countries.
3. **Turn it into a share of the world.** Twelve countries is a bit over 6% of the 195. Our guide to [how many countries you have visited](/blog/how-many-countries-have-i-visited) shows how to work out your own percentage.

If you want to compare continents, see how the other counts work: [Europe](/blog/how-many-countries-in-europe) and [Africa](/blog/how-many-countries-in-africa) each have their own list.

## FAQ

### How many countries are in South America?

There are 12 sovereign countries in South America: Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Chile, Colombia, Ecuador, Guyana, Paraguay, Peru, Suriname, Uruguay and Venezuela.

### Is French Guiana a country?

No. French Guiana is an overseas region and department of France, so it is part of France and the European Union. It is on the South American continent but does not count as a country.

### Which South American countries are landlocked?

Bolivia and Paraguay are the two landlocked countries in South America. All the others have a coastline.

### Which country in South America speaks Dutch?

Suriname. It is the only South American country where Dutch is the main official language.

### Is Mexico in South America?

No. Mexico is in North America. South America starts south of Panama, and Colombia is its northernmost country on the mainland.
