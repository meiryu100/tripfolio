There are **32 NATO countries** as of 2026. Twelve founded the alliance in 1949, and 20 more have joined since, most recently Finland in 2023 and Sweden in 2024. Below is the full alphabetical list with the year each country joined, the enlargement rounds in order, which members are also in the EU, and how to keep track of them on a map.

The alliance is built on the North Atlantic Treaty, signed in Washington, D.C. on 4 April 1949. It entered into force on 24 August 1949, according to the [text of the treaty on nato.int](https://www.nato.int/en/about-us/official-texts-and-resources/official-texts/1949/04/04/the-north-atlantic-treaty).

## Who were the 12 founding members?

The founders were Belgium, Canada, Denmark, France, Iceland, Italy, Luxembourg, the Netherlands, Norway, Portugal, the United Kingdom and the United States. That is ten European countries and two North American ones, which is why the alliance is often described as a transatlantic one.

## How did NATO grow from 12 to 32?

Membership grew in eleven steps. The dates below come from Wikipedia's [member states of NATO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Member_states_of_NATO) page, and the countries match the official list above:

1. **1949:** the 12 founders.
2. **1952:** Greece and Türkiye (18 February).
3. **1955:** West Germany (6 May).
4. **1982:** Spain (30 May).
5. **1999:** Czechia, Hungary and Poland (12 March).
6. **2004:** Bulgaria, Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania, Romania, Slovakia and Slovenia (29 March), the biggest single round.
7. **2009:** Albania and Croatia (1 April).
8. **2017:** Montenegro (5 June).
9. **2020:** North Macedonia (27 March).
10. **2023:** Finland (4 April).
11. **2024:** Sweden (7 March).

Add it up: 12 + 2 + 1 + 1 + 3 + 7 + 2 + 1 + 1 + 1 + 1 = 32.

One detail trips people up. Germany's 1990 reunification did not add a new member. East Germany became part of an existing member, so the count stayed the same.

### Which countries joined most recently?

Finland (4 April 2023) and Sweden (7 March 2024). Both are also on the map as Northern European countries, and both are easy additions to a travel list: see [Finland](/countries/finland) and [Sweden](/countries/sweden).

## How does a country join NATO?

Under Article 10 of the treaty, the members can, by unanimous agreement, invite another European state that can further the treaty's principles and contribute to the security of the North Atlantic area. The invited country becomes a party once it deposits its instrument of accession with the U.S. Government. In practice, that means every current member has to agree, so a single country can hold up an invitation.

## What is Article 5?

Article 5 is the collective-defence clause. It says that an armed attack against one or more members in Europe or North America is considered an attack against them all, and that each member may respond, including with armed force, to restore and maintain security in the North Atlantic area. The treaty leaves each member to decide what action it takes.

## Can a country leave NATO?

Article 13 allows a member to leave once the treaty has been in force for 20 years, with the withdrawal taking effect one year after notice is given to the U.S. Government. No country has ever left. France is sometimes mentioned here, but it only stepped out of NATO's integrated military command from 1966 to 2009. It stayed a member the whole time.

## Which NATO countries are not in the EU, and which EU countries are not in NATO?

The two clubs overlap, but they are not the same. Comparing the NATO list above with the [27 EU members](/blog/countries-in-the-european-union):

- **23 countries are in both.**
- **9 NATO members are not in the EU:** Albania, Canada, Iceland, Montenegro, North Macedonia, Norway, Türkiye, the United Kingdom and the United States.
- **4 EU members are not in NATO:** Austria, Cyprus, Ireland and Malta.

The counts (23, 9, 4) are our own comparison of the two official lists, so check them again if either organization adds a member.

## Which continents do NATO countries cover?

Using the continents on the Tripfolio map, the 32 members split like this:

- **Europe: 29**
- **North America: 2** (Canada and the United States)
- **Asia: 1** (Türkiye)

Türkiye is the one that surprises people. The alliance's treaty area is about Europe and North America, and Türkiye has been a member since 1952, but Tripfolio files it under Asia (Western Asia), as the UN geoscheme does. The same question comes up in [how many countries are in Europe](/blog/how-many-countries-in-europe), and [how many continents there are](/blog/how-many-continents-are-there) explains why borders like this depend on the model you pick. If you want to plan a visit, [Türkiye](/countries/turkiye) and [Iceland](/countries/iceland) are two members at very different corners of the alliance.

## Which countries want to join NATO?

Bosnia and Herzegovina, Georgia and Ukraine are described on Wikipedia as having formally expressed aspirations to join. The 2008 Bucharest Summit said Georgia and Ukraine "will become members of NATO in the future", and Bosnia and Herzegovina was invited to the Membership Action Plan in April 2010. None of them is a member today, so none counts in the 32.

## FAQ

### How many countries are in NATO?

There are 32 member countries. 29 are in Europe, two in North America (Canada and the United States) and one, Türkiye, is in Asia on the Tripfolio map.

### Which countries were the founding members of NATO?

Belgium, Canada, Denmark, France, Iceland, Italy, Luxembourg, the Netherlands, Norway, Portugal, the United Kingdom and the United States. They signed the North Atlantic Treaty in Washington, D.C. on 4 April 1949.

### What are the newest NATO members?

Finland joined on 4 April 2023 and Sweden on 7 March 2024. Sweden is the latest member.

### Is Ukraine in NATO?

No. Ukraine has said it wants to join, and the 2008 Bucharest Summit statement said it will become a member in the future, but it is not one of the 32 members.

### Is Switzerland in NATO?

No. Switzerland is not on NATO's list of 32 member countries.
