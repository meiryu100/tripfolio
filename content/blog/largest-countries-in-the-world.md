The largest country in the world is Russia, with about 17.1 million km² of total area. Canada is second at roughly 10 million km², followed by China, the United States and Brazil, and the top ten ends with Australia, India, Argentina, Kazakhstan and Algeria. The order of the middle of the list depends on how area is measured, and that is the part most pages skip.

This guide ranks the largest countries in the world, shows which continent each one is on, explains why China and the United States swap places in some lists, and points out what the numbers mean for anyone counting countries.

## What are the 10 largest countries in the world?

Here is the ranking by total area (land plus inland water), using the figures in [Wikipedia's list of countries and dependencies by area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_countries_and_dependencies_by_area). It takes total area mainly from United Nations Statistics Division data. Continents follow the way Tripfolio files each country.

1. **Russia**: 17,098,246 km² (Europe)
2. **Canada**: 9,984,670 km² (North America)
3. **China**: 9,596,960 km² (Asia)
4. **United States**: 9,525,067 km² (North America)
5. **Brazil**: 8,510,346 km² (South America)
6. **Australia**: 7,741,220 km² (Oceania)
7. **India**: 3,287,263 km² (Asia)
8. **Argentina**: 2,780,400 km² (South America)
9. **Kazakhstan**: 2,724,910 km² (Asia)
10. **Algeria**: 2,381,741 km² (Africa)

Places 11 and 12 on the same list are the Democratic Republic of the Congo (2,344,858 km²) and Saudi Arabia (2,149,690 km²).

Two things stand out. First, Russia is nearly 1.7 times the size of Canada, the runner-up. Second, there is a huge drop after Australia: the sixth country is more than twice the size of the seventh. Only six countries are bigger than 7 million km².

## Which is bigger: China or the United States?

It depends on the source. In the Wikipedia table above, China (9,596,960 km²) ranks third and the United States (9,525,067 km²) fourth, and China is also ahead on land area alone (9,326,410 km² against 9,147,593 km²). Other lists, including some that rely on the CIA World Factbook, put the United States third.

The gap is under one percent, so the difference comes from how coastal waters, lakes and disputed borders are treated, not from anything on the ground. Wikipedia itself says the CIA World Factbook is most often used when different UN departments disagree. If you need one answer, "about the same size, with China slightly ahead on UN figures" is the honest one.

## What is the difference between total area and land area?

Total area is land plus inland water such as lakes and rivers. Land area leaves the water out. Most "largest country" lists use total area, and Wikipedia's list does too, taking its land and water splits from the Food and Agriculture Organization.

The two measures matter mostly for countries with a lot of lakes:

- **Russia**: 17,098,246 km² total, 16,376,870 km² land. It leads on both.
- **Canada**: 9,984,670 km² total but 9,093,507 km² land. About 891,000 km² of the difference is inland water.
- **China**: 9,596,960 km² total, 9,326,410 km² land. With the smaller water share, China is **ahead of Canada on land area alone**.
- **Algeria and Saudi Arabia**: no inland water in the table, so total and land are identical.

So if the question is "which country has the most dry land", the second place changes: China comes ahead of Canada. The first place does not.

## Which continents have the largest countries?

Using the way Tripfolio files countries:

- **Asia** has three of the ten: China, India and Kazakhstan.
- **North America** has two: Canada and the United States.
- **South America** has two: Brazil and Argentina.
- **Europe**, **Oceania** and **Africa** have one each: Russia, Australia and Algeria.

Russia spans Europe and Asia, so continent labels for it are a choice. Tripfolio files it under Europe. Kazakhstan is a similar border case but is filed under Asia.

## Which large countries are worth a deep dive?

If a country is huge, one trip rarely covers it. Brazil, Australia and Canada are good examples of countries where two people can both say "I've been" and have seen completely different places. Our pages for [Brazil](/countries/brazil), [Canada](/countries/canada) and [Australia](/countries/australia) have the basics on each.

A practical way to think about it:

1. Count the country once, using one consistent rule. Our guide on [what counts as visiting a country](/blog/what-counts-as-visiting-a-country) lays out the common ones.
2. Use regions to record how much of it you have really seen, as with US states.
3. Use trips with dates and photos to remember where you actually went.

## Are the biggest countries also the most populous?

Not always, and area and population are different questions. India is seventh by area but is a very large country by population, while Canada and Australia are enormous by area with comparatively small populations. If you are planning around size, check an official population source for the figure you need rather than assuming that a bigger country has more people.

## How do the largest countries compare with the smallest?

At the other end of the list is Vatican City, at roughly half a square kilometer. Our guide to the [smallest countries in the world](/blog/smallest-countries-in-the-world) ranks the 15 tiniest. Russia is tens of millions of times larger than Vatican City, yet both count exactly once. On a country-counting map, a tiny state and a giant one are worth the same: Tripfolio counts the 195 sovereign countries toward "x / 195", and our post on [how many countries there are in the world](/blog/how-many-countries-are-there-in-the-world) explains where that number comes from.

The area list is also a reminder of what is not a country. Wikipedia lists Western Sahara (about 266,000 km²) as disputed and unranked, and Antarctica (about 14.2 million km² of land) sits outside the country ranking altogether, as it has no countries.

## FAQ

### What is the largest country in the world?

Russia, with about 17.1 million km² of total area, according to UN-based figures. It is the largest by land area as well.

### What is the second-largest country in the world?

Canada, with about 9.98 million km² of total area. Measured by land area alone, China comes ahead of Canada because Canada has far more inland water.

### Is Russia bigger than Antarctica?

Russia's total area is larger than Antarctica's land area of about 14.2 million km². Antarctica isn't a country, so it isn't ranked in lists of countries.

### Which is the largest country in Africa?

Algeria, at about 2.38 million km². It is tenth in the world.

### Is the United States or China bigger?

The two are within about one percent of each other. On the UN-based figures in Wikipedia's list, China is slightly larger, while some other lists put the United States third.
