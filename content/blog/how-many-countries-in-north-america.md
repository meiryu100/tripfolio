There are **23 countries in North America**. That includes the three big ones (Canada, the United States and Mexico), the seven countries of Central America and the 13 island nations of the Caribbean. On top of those, the United Nations lists 18 territories in the same part of the world, such as Greenland, Puerto Rico and Bermuda. They are not independent countries, so they do not add to the 23.

If you have seen the number 3, or 12, or 35 somewhere, that is not a mistake so much as a different definition of "North America". This guide gives the full list, shows where the definitions split and explains how to count it on your own travel map.

## How many countries are in North America?

The United Nations Statistics Division groups the Americas into regions in its [standard country and area codes](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/methodology/m49/). Three of those regions together make up what most people call North America:

- **Northern America**: 5 entries
- **Central America**: 8 entries
- **Caribbean**: 28 entries

That is 41 places in total. Of those, **23 are sovereign states** and **18 are territories** that belong to another country (or, in Greenland's case, are self-governing within one). [Wikipedia's North America article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_America) also gives 23 sovereign states, which matches.

## Which places in North America are not countries?

The UN lists 18 territories in this part of the world. Three sit in Northern America and 15 in the Caribbean.

### Northern America

- **Bermuda**, a British Overseas Territory in the North Atlantic
- **Greenland**, a self-governing territory within the Kingdom of Denmark
- **Saint Pierre and Miquelon**, a French territory off the coast of Canada

### The Caribbean

- **United States:** Puerto Rico, United States Virgin Islands
- **France:** Guadeloupe, Martinique, Saint Barthélemy, Saint Martin
- **Netherlands:** Aruba, Curaçao, Sint Maarten, and Bonaire, Sint Eustatius and Saba
- **United Kingdom:** Anguilla, British Virgin Islands, Cayman Islands, Montserrat, Turks and Caicos Islands

Greenland is the one people ask about most. [Wikipedia describes it](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greenland) as part of the continent of North America and as a self-governing territory in the Kingdom of Denmark, with self-rule since 2009. Politically it belongs with Denmark, so it is not a 24th country.

## Why do some lists say 3, 12 or 35?

Most of the disagreement comes from four different meanings of "North America".

### Three countries: the trade-bloc version

Wikipedia notes that "North America" in trade agreements such as NAFTA means only Canada, Mexico and the United States. That is where the answer "3" comes from. It is a political grouping, not a geographic one.

### Northern America only

Some sources, including the UN's "Northern America" group, mean just the northern part: Canada and the United States, plus Bermuda, Greenland and Saint Pierre and Miquelon. In that sense there are two countries.

### The Americas as one continent

In many countries, including most of Latin America and parts of Europe, students learn that there is one continent called America, not two. In that model, North America is a subcontinent, and the country count depends on where you draw the line at Panama and Colombia. The UN does not use that model for its regional lists, and neither does Tripfolio, which uses seven continents.

### Territories counted as countries

Puerto Rico, Aruba and Greenland all have their own flags and tourism boards, and many travel sites list them as countries. If you add all 18 territories to the 23, you get 41 places. This is the same reason that the world total is 195 and not 250: the rule is that a [sovereign country counts and a dependency does not](/blog/how-many-countries-are-there-in-the-world).

## Where does Central America end?

Panama is the last country of North America on the usual definition, and Colombia is the first of South America. Panama is on the Central American isthmus, so it belongs to North America in the UN list. If you want the other side of that border, our guide to [how many countries there are in South America](/blog/how-many-countries-in-south-america) has the 12 on the other side.

Belize is another one people get wrong. It is on the mainland, has a Caribbean coast and is a member of the Caribbean Community, but the UN files it under Central America. Either way it is part of the 23.

## Which number should I use for my own count?

Pick a rule and stay with it:

1. **23, sovereign states only.** The simplest rule, and the one Tripfolio uses. It keeps your count consistent with the world total of 195.
2. **23 plus territories.** Count the 23 as countries and keep Greenland, Puerto Rico and the rest as bonus places next to them.
3. **A personal rule.** Some travelers only count a place if they stayed a night or left the airport. If you are unsure where you stand, read [what counts as visiting a country](/blog/what-counts-as-visiting-a-country).

North America is a good example of how a big continent can hide a small number. The region covers about 24.7 million square kilometres, according to Wikipedia, but 13 of its 23 countries are small island nations. Someone who has driven across Canada and the United States has two countries. Someone who has island-hopped the Lesser Antilles may have collected six.

## FAQ

### How many countries are in North America?

There are 23 sovereign countries in North America: Canada, the United States, Mexico, seven in Central America and 13 in the Caribbean. The UN also lists 18 territories in the same region, which are not counted as countries.

### Is Mexico in North America or Central America?

Both, depending on the source. Geographically and in everyday use Mexico is part of North America, along with Canada and the United States. The UN files it under Central America, which it treats as running from Mexico to Panama. It is one of the 23 countries either way.

### Is Greenland a country?

No. Greenland is a self-governing territory within the Kingdom of Denmark, and it has had self-rule since 2009. It is part of the North American continent geographically, but it is not counted among the 23 countries.

### Is the Caribbean part of North America?

Yes. The UN lists the Caribbean as part of the Americas, and under the usual six-continent model its 13 countries are counted as part of North America. Panama and the rest of Central America are included as well.

### Why do some sources say North America has only three countries?

That comes from trade agreements such as NAFTA, which involved only Canada, Mexico and the United States. It describes a trade bloc, not the continent, which has 23 sovereign states.
